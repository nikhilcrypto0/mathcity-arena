# AI Server Factories Are Hiring Debug Technicians Now

The strongest 2026 demand for debug, diagnostic, test and rack-integration technicians in the US comes from about a dozen AI-server factories, and most of them post pay that reaches the $23–35/hr band. **Pay is highest in Silicon Valley and at a few established OEM lines in the Midwest and Southeast.** In California, Supermicro posts $25–30/hr, Sanmina $23–31/hr, Quanta $26–28/hr, and an Aerotek server-repair contract pays $25.50–29.50/hr depending on shift. Lenovo in Whitsett, NC posts $26–32/hr, HPE in Chippewa Falls, WI posts a $21.83–46.25/hr range, and ZT Systems (now part of Sanmina) in Secaucus, NJ posts $28.50/hr. **The largest number of openings is in Texas**, at Foxconn Houston, Wistron Fort Worth and Wiwynn El Paso. Those plants start at $18–25/hr and reach $25–29/hr only at the top of their ranges or on nights. Jabil Florence, KY, the user's benchmark, has paid $24–27.60/hr through Aerotek, but that posting dates from August 2025 and has closed. Jabil's next large wave is its new plant in Salisbury, NC. Staffing agencies matter most at Jabil (Aerotek, Lorien and Kelly are all documented there) and on the data-center side. There, TEKsystems, Insight Global, Apex, Lorien, Introl and GSD Telecom fill contract roles at $22–49/hr. The Taiwanese manufacturers (Foxconn, Wistron, Wiwynn, Quanta, Inventec, Compal) mostly hire directly. For an F-1 student, regular OPT allows W-2 agency work if the job relates to the degree. STEM OPT is much harder through an agency: the agency must be enrolled in E-Verify and must itself sign and carry out the Form I-983 training plan. That risk grew in 2026, when the government launched enforcement aimed at staffing firms that employ OPT students.

## OPT works through W-2 agencies; STEM OPT through an agency is fragile

**Regular post-completion OPT** allows employment through a staffing agency, temp agency or contract firm. The job must be directly related to the major and average at least 20 hours per week ([UTA ISSS](https://uta.edu/student-affairs/oie/isss/employment/opt-categories-simplified/reporting); [Harvard HIO](https://www.hio.harvard.edu/node/50457)). Unemployment is capped at **90 days** during the initial 12-month OPT, and the days keep counting during gaps between contracts ([Temple ISSS](https://global.temple.edu/isss/students/current-students/f-1-student/f-1-employment-options/campus-employment/optional-practical-training-opt/post-completion-opt/opt-and-unemployment)). Within 10 days of starting or of any change, you must report the employer, the actual work address, the dates, and how the job relates to your field in the SEVP Portal ([Study in the States](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/fm-student-employment/f-1-add-edit-delete-optional-practical)). With an agency, the agency is the employer, and the client factory is normally the worksite to report. Confirm that convention with your DSO.

Whether a job is "directly related" decides this whole route, and it is judged case by case. **Server debug, test and failure analysis fit EE and electronics degrees most naturally.** CS degrees fit when the duties stress Linux, BIOS/firmware or test scripting. IS degrees fit data-center operations moderately. Purely physical rack-and-stack or "no experience needed" roles are the weakest case, especially for a master's graduate ([migratemate](https://migratemate.co/opt-jobs/data-center-technician); [JHU sample explanations](https://ois.jhu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/sample_OPT_explanations.pdf)). In practice, choose postings titled Debug, Diagnostic, Test or Repair Technician over Operator or Assembler. Get the duties in writing so your SEVP Portal explanation can cite them.

**STEM OPT through an agency is legal but conditional.** The agency must be enrolled in E-Verify. It must be the bona fide employer. Its own staff must provide and supervise the training on Form I-983, which it signs. It must pay what similarly situated US workers earn ([SJSU ISSS](https://www.sjsu.edu/isss/current-students/employment/stem/employment.php); [Study in the States DSO Q&A](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/2016/07/questions-designated-school-officials-can-stem-opt-students-use-staffing-or-temporary-0)). The agency **cannot hand training to the client's staff**. A client line lead acting as your real trainer is the classic compliance failure ([USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-extension-for-stem-students-stem-opt)). Training may take place at the client site as long as the agency keeps those duties ([Gokare Law](https://gokarelaw.com/uscis-clarifies-that-stem-opt-students-may-engage-in-training-at-third-party-locations/)). ICE can make site visits, normally with 48 hours' notice, or with no notice when there is a complaint ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/stem-opt-worksite-inspections-what-employers-need-to-know.html)). If the client converts you to its own payroll, that is a new employer: you need a new I-983 and the client must be enrolled in E-Verify. The overall unemployment limit across OPT plus STEM OPT is widely understood to be 150 days; confirm it on the [STEM OPT Hub](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/stem-opt-hub/additional-resources/stem-opt-extension-overview) with your DSO. **Kforce is the only agency found that publicly markets E-Verify participation for STEM OPT** ([Kforce](https://www.kforce.com/find-work/visa-sponsorship-solutions/)). For any other agency, check the [E-Verify Employer Search](https://www.e-verify.gov/e-verify-employer-search) and ask the recruiter. Enrollment alone does not mean the agency will sign an I-983.

The 2026 policy climate raises the stakes. On March 23, 2026, SEVP issued Broadcast Message 2603-02, which targets fraud by "IT recruitment, consulting firms, and staffing agencies" ([ICE](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/pdf/bcm260302.pdf)). In May, ICE said it had found more than 10,000 students claiming employment with "highly suspect employers" ([Adhikari Law](https://www.adhikarilaw.com/news-and-alerts/news-insights/trump-administration-news-insights/immigration-and-customs-enforcement-ice-releases-information-on-alleged-fraud-related-to-the-24-month-opt-extension-for-stem-f-1-students/)). A rule replacing "duration of status" with fixed admission periods took effect September 15, 2026 ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/17/2026-14439/establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and-an-extension-of-stay-procedure-for-nonimmigrant)). An OPT fee proposal cleared White House review September 11, 2026 but has not been published ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-dhs-optional-practical-training-fee-proposal-clears-federal-review.html)). The safest choice is a large agency with real US offices, US payroll and real supervision, such as Aerotek/TEKsystems/Actalent (all owned by Allegis Group), Insight Global, Kforce or Apex. Small "OPT consulting" firms are what the enforcement targets.

Some sites screen OPT holders out entirely. Sanmina Houston (ITAR, citizens and permanent residents only), Supermicro's Jersey City test roles (ITAR) ([freehire](https://freehire.me/jobs/test-engineer-supermicro-xbdcf6aa)) and AWS GovCloud facilities ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/itar/)) are closed. Postings that say "without sponsorship now or in the future" are a judgment call. If your EAD covers the start date, you can truthfully say you are authorized to work. If you will need a visa later, answer the "future sponsorship" question honestly. Misrepresentation is far riskier than disclosure ([TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/blog/how-to-answer-sponsorship-question)). Hourly technician jobs rarely lead to H-1B sponsorship, especially with a proposed $103,265 H-1B fee pending ([Fakhoury Law](https://fakhouryglobal.com/immigration-alerts/fgi-alert-dhs-proposes-permanent-103265-h-1b-fee-potentially-expanding-costs-for-opt-to-h-1b-sponsorship/)). Plan on the OPT window being the whole horizon unless the job leads to an engineering role.

### Recruiter question checklist

| Ask the recruiter | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Who is the client, and what is the exact worksite address? | You report the worksite in the SEVP Portal, and it lets you check for ITAR or federal work |
| Is the program ITAR, export-controlled, federal or GovCloud? Does the client accept EAD holders? | These sites exclude OPT holders. Find out before the background check, not after |
| Will I be your W-2 employee (not 1099 or C2C)? How often is pay, and what are the overtime rules? | OPT employment must be real employment; W-2 is the clean structure |
| How many guaranteed hours per week, which shift, and what is the shift differential? | You need 20+ hours/week for OPT; night shift is the fastest raise |
| Can you send the job duties in writing (debug, diagnostics, BIOS/BMC, Linux, test)? | This is your evidence for the "directly related to my degree" explanation |
| How long is the contract, how often are extensions given, and when and how does conversion happen? | Gaps count against the 90-day (OPT) or 150-day (STEM OPT) unemployment limit |
| If this assignment ends early, will you place me somewhere else, and how fast? | Same reason: unemployment days keep counting between contracts |
| Is your company enrolled in E-Verify under the legal name and EIN that will appear on my I-983? | Required for STEM OPT; the EIN must match the I-983 |
| Will you sign Form I-983, and who at your company (not the client) will supervise and evaluate me? | Training cannot be delegated to the client's staff |
| If the client converts me, is the client enrolled in E-Verify and willing to sign a new I-983? | Conversion is an employer change that needs a new I-983 and new reporting |
| Is my rate the same as US workers in this role? What benefits do you offer (health, PTO, 401k)? | STEM OPT requires pay matching similarly situated US workers |
| What is the timeline for the background check, drug screen and E-Verify, compared with my EAD start date? | You cannot start before the EAD start date |

## A six-week preparation plan aimed at debug interviews

Job posts point to a three-tier skill ladder. **Entry factory test** roles need SOP discipline, ESD practice, Windows and Windows Server, and basic networking. Foxconn's Houston test technician post asks only for secondary education and technical English ([ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/c/Foxconn-Industrial-Internet-FII/Job/Test-Technician-(G-Project)/-in-Houston,TX?jid=996014d4a6c3c330)). **Debug and diagnostic** roles add Linux diagnostics (dmesg, journalctl, lspci, dmidecode), BIOS setup and firmware flashing, BMC/IPMI, SEL log collection, burn-in and stress testing, and validating CPU, memory, storage, NIC and GPU subsystems ([Synnex posting](https://www.dreamworkhq.com/job/a69d99dd-276a-487f-ad34-a31d4b23c037)). **Failure analysis and RMA** roles add reading schematics, oscilloscopes and logic analyzers, and component-level repair, and they pay up to $35–45/hr with experience ([ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Failure-Analysis-Technician)). For AI racks, fiber discipline sets candidates apart: inspect, clean and re-inspect every MPO connector before mating ([Leviathan Systems](https://www.leviathansystems.co/blog/gb300-nvl72-deployment-guide)). That comes from an integrator guide, not a job post.

| Week | Action | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Linux Foundation LFS101 (free, Credly badge) ([LF](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/training/introduction-to-linux/)). Install Ubuntu on an old PC and practice lspci, dmidecode, dmesg, lsblk and journalctl daily | $0 |
| 1–2 | OSHA 10 General Industry online ([OSHA.com](https://www.osha.com/blog/osha-10-30-cost)). Start applying to the tier-1 shortlist and to Aerotek and Lorien searches now; don't wait for certificates | ~$60 |
| 2–3 | Professor Messer A+ Core 1 hardware videos ([Professor Messer](https://www.professormesser.com/)). Drill no-POST triage and DIMM isolation | $0 |
| 3 | LPI Linux Essentials exam ([LPI](https://www.lpi.org/exam-pricing/)) | $120 |
| 4–5 | NVIDIA DLI "Introduction to AI in the Data Center," then the NCA-AIIO exam (about 20 hours of study reported) ([DevOpsCube](https://devopscube.com/nvidia-certified-ai-infra-and-operations/)) | $125–150 |
| 5–6 | FOA Fiber U self-study, then the CFOT exam ([FiberCareer](https://fibercareer.com/cfot-certification-guide)). Practice ipmitool on any used server with a BMC | ~$70 |
| Optional | CompTIA A+ (two exams at $274 each since June 2026) or Server+ ($399), only if target postings name them ([Total Seminars](https://totalsem.com/comptia-exam-price-change-2026/)). Leave IPC-A-610 and J-STD-001 ($650–1,500) for the employer to fund | $399–548 |

Interviews mix scenario troubleshooting with safety and basic networking, usually an HR screen followed by technical rounds ([The Interview Guys](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/data-center-technician-interview-questions/)). Prepare spoken answers to these:

| Interview question | What a strong answer covers |
|---|---|
| A server fails POST. What do you check? | Power and PSU, a minimal configuration, POST codes, the BMC SEL and fault LEDs, then swap DIMM, CPU or board one part at a time |
| How do you identify a failed DIMM? | Reseat, run memory tests, boot with one DIMM, then add sticks back one at a time to separate a bad slot from a bad DIMM ([Oracle docs](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E20815_01/html/E20840/xdiag.glaep.html)) |
| How do you check a node without touching it? | Out-of-band access through IPMI or iDRAC: power state, SEL entries, sensor readings (`ipmitool sel list`, `ipmitool sdr`, `ipmitool chassis status`) |
| Which Linux commands show hardware faults? | `dmesg \| grep -i error`, `journalctl -k`, `lspci -vv`, `dmidecode -t memory`, `lsblk`, `nvidia-smi` |
| How do you prevent ESD damage? | Wrist strap, mat, grounding checks, handling boards by the edges, ESD bags |
| How do you handle fiber in a GPU rack? | Inspect with a scope, clean, re-inspect before every mating; handle MPO connectors and dust caps carefully |
| Describe a fault you root-caused. | The symptom, the tests that narrowed it down, the root cause, the fix, and the corrective action you documented |

Resume keywords should mirror posting language exactly: **debug, diagnostics, root cause analysis (RCA), BIOS configuration, firmware flashing, BMC/IPMI, SEL log analysis, burn-in/stress/functional testing, CPU/memory/storage/NIC/GPU validation, Linux (dmesg, lspci, dmidecode, journalctl), Windows Server, ESD compliance, test station setup, rack integration, cabling, MPO fiber inspection, schematics, oscilloscope, RMA, failure analysis**. Write certifications out by their full names ([Indeed FA posting](https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=b92b53211f66c9bb)). Add "NVIDIA HGX" or "GB200 NVL72" only if you have genuine lab or course exposure.

## Conclusion

The market has shifted. The best-documented $23–35/hr debug work is no longer at Jabil Florence. It is at established OEM and EMS lines (Supermicro, Sanmina, Lenovo, HPE, ZT Systems) and at the next ramp: Jabil Salisbury, Foxconn Mount Pleasant and the North Texas plants. Florence is now mainly a reference point for how agencies like Aerotek and Lorien structure these contracts. For an F-1 graduate, the binding constraint is not finding a posting but finding one that is degree-related, non-ITAR, through a W-2 employer, and, for STEM OPT, signed by an E-Verify employer that supervises your training. That favors applying **directly** to manufacturers wherever possible, since a direct hire makes the I-983 straightforward. Use agencies mainly on regular OPT, or with Kforce and other agencies that confirm in writing that they will sign an I-983.

The most practical sequence is to apply now to the five tier-1 targets and to Aerotek "debug / repair test / configuration" searches in Fremont, Florence, Salisbury and Fort Worth. Use night shift to get into the band at the Texas plants. Within 1–2 years of debug experience, aim to move into Test Engineer or Failure Analysis Engineer roles ($65K–125K) ([Indeed Supermicro](https://www.indeed.com/q-supermicro-engineer-l-san-jose,-ca-jobs.html)), which strengthens the degree-relatedness case. Do not finalize any choice until your DSO has reviewed the written job duties and you have confirmed the posting is still live.
