# US EMS / Server-OEM Sites Building and Testing AI Servers and Racks: Technician Hiring (as of Oct 2, 2026)

Method note: Research used WebSearch snippets only. Direct fetches of maktinta.com, celestica.com and jobs.aerotek.com were all blocked by the egress proxy, so the pay figures below come from search-engine summaries of job posts and salary pages. Glassdoor, Indeed and ZipRecruiter "estimated pay" figures are self-reported or modeled. They are not posted pay, and they are labeled as such below. Job-board page titles carry a month stamp (for example "Sep 2026"), which shows when the board page was crawled, not when a specific job was posted.

## Q1. Which US cities does each company integrate/test servers or racks in, and what hiring scale (announced jobs, hiring events) in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The biggest 2025–2027 US build-outs for AI server and rack integration are concentrated in North Texas, Houston, Wisconsin, North Carolina, Northern Kentucky and Silicon Valley:
- Celestica in Fort Worth: about 1,700 jobs
- Wistron in Fort Worth: 800+ jobs (500+ already hired)
- Jabil in Rowan County, NC: 1,181 jobs, ramping from mid-2026
- Foxconn in Mount Pleasant, WI: 1,374 jobs
- Jabil in Florence, KY: 900 jobs (hiring event Jan 2025)
- Foxconn in Houston: "hundreds" of jobs
- Supermicro in San Jose: a new 714k sq ft campus

Established test and integration lines that hire continuously run at Celestica Richardson TX, Sanmina Fremont CA / Huntsville AL / Kenosha WI, Sanmina (ex-ZT Systems) Secaucus NJ / Georgetown TX, HPE Chippewa Falls WI, Lenovo Whitsett NC, and Supermicro San Jose/Fremont.

Most debug, test and repair technician pay at these sites falls between $22 and $32/hr. California is the top of the range ($25–36/hr at Supermicro and Sanmina). Aerotek's Fremont server repair post pays $25.50 days, $27.00 swing and $29.50 graveyard.

## Q4. Any evidence about OPT/F-1 hiring or work authorization language?

### Takeaway
No posting found explicitly welcomes F-1 OPT for these technician roles. Several postings restrict work authorization:
- Sanmina Houston: ITAR facility, US citizen or permanent resident only
- Supermicro Sr. Service Technician: "authorization to work in the U.S. without sponsorship"
- Supermicro Jersey City Test Engineer: US citizen or green card holder due to ITAR

Standard commercial (non-ITAR) EMS lines generally only require authorization to work. OPT holders with an EAD meet that, if the employer accepts them. Some agency postings also require the work to relate to the degree.

  - OPT holders can work for any US employer, at least part-time, if the work relates to their major. — [Dartmouth OVIS](https://ovis-intl.dartmouth.edu/info-depts/hiring-f-1-opt-and-stem-opt-employees)
