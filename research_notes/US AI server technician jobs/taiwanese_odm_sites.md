# US Factory Sites of Taiwanese ODMs / Server Integrators Hiring AI-Server Debug, Test and Rack-Integration Technicians (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: Research done 2026-10-02 using WebSearch only. Direct page fetch was tried once (keranews.org) and blocked by the egress proxy, so most facts come from search-result snippets of job boards (Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter) and news pages. Job-board snippets are aggregator summaries: individual postings may have closed, and pay ranges may mix several postings. Where a search engine's summary looked garbled, this is flagged. The benchmark is Jabil Florence, KY (Aerotek Debug Technician $24–27.60/hr; Lorien "Computer Hardware Diagnostic Technician" $22–26/hr).

## Q1. Which US cities/states does each company build or test AI servers/racks in, and at what hiring scale (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
The biggest hiring centers are in **Texas**: Foxconn in Houston, Wistron in Fort Worth (Alliance), Wiwynn in Socorro/El Paso, Inventec in Katy, and Pegatron and Compal in Georgetown/Taylor. Outside Texas, the main sites are **Foxconn in Mount Pleasant, WI** (about 1,374 jobs announced Nov 2025, plus 700 more by mid-2027 announced Sep 2026) and **Foxconn/SoftBank in Lordstown, OH** (1,600–2,000 jobs, Aug 2025). Quanta runs established server factories in **Fremont, CA** and **La Vergne (Nashville), TN**. Each site has announced hundreds to a few thousand jobs. Several plants opened or started mass production in 2026.

## Q2. Which technician job titles are posted, and at what hourly pay (target $23–35/hr)? Shift differentials?

### Takeaway
Direct-hire technician pay at these ODMs mostly runs **$18–29/hr**, so it overlaps the Jabil/Aerotek benchmark ($24–27.60). The best-documented postings are:
- Foxconn FII Houston Test Technician, Overnight: $21–28.75/hr
- Foxconn Houston Debug Technician: $18–28/hr
- Wiwynn Socorro Test Technician: $25/hr on night shift
- Quanta Fremont Repair Technician: $26–28/hr
- Inventec Katy Engineering Technician: about $23.50–28.80/hr

Night shifts appear common, with pay at the top of each range. No explicit "rack integration technician" or "L10/L11 technician" postings surfaced in search snippets.

### Takeaway
I found **no search-visible evidence** naming a staffing agency (Aerotek, Lorien, Kelly, Randstad, Actalent, Insight Global and others) recruiting for a specific Taiwanese ODM AI-server site. ### Gaps
