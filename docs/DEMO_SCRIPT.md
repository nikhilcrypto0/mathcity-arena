## One-click alternative

If presenting solo, click **Demo Mode → Launch Full Demo Match**: 6 teams and 18
synthetic players play the entire match above through the real engine in ~90 seconds,
with Pause / Next Event / 1×-2×-4× / Reset controls. Beats 8-19 all occur on screen.
